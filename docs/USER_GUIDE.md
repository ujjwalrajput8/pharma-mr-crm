# Jovance Field Force CRM — User Guide

Last updated: 2 November 2026

## 1. Getting started

This guide explains every screen of the Jovance Field Force CRM in simple words: what you see on each screen and what each button does. You will only see the screens your role is allowed to use.

### How to log in

1. Open the app link in your browser. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Click the eye icon in the password box to see what you typed.
3. If you enter a wrong password **5 times**, your account is **locked for 15 minutes**. Wait 15 minutes and try again.
4. Forgot your password? The login page says "Forgot password? Contact your administrator". Your Admin will create a **reset link** from your employee profile and give it to you. Open that link to set a new password.

### The screen layout

- **Sidebar (left side):** all screens are listed here in groups: Overview, Field, People, Masters, Stock, Commercial and System. You can collapse the sidebar to get more working space. On a mobile phone, open the sidebar with the menu button at the top.
- **Search (Ctrl + K):** press **Ctrl + K** on the keyboard and type the name of a screen, a doctor or a product. Use the arrow keys to pick one and press Enter.
- **Notifications (bell icon):** new tasks and updates appear here, for example a decision on your leave. The red number shows how many are unread. "Mark all read" clears them.
- **Dark / Light mode:** the moon or sun button changes the colour theme. The app remembers your choice.
- **Profile menu:** click your name at the top right. It has **My Profile** (your details, leave balance, change password) and **Logout**. The app asks you to confirm before logging out.
- **JL loader:** while data is loading, the Jovance "JL" logo moves on the screen. It disappears as soon as the data arrives.

## 2. Dashboard

The Dashboard opens right after you log in. It shows today's work, pending items and the last 30 days at a glance. From top to bottom:

### Greeting card

At the top you see "Good morning / afternoon / evening" with your name and today's date. Small chips below show how many appointments you have today and how many visits have been logged. MRs and Managers also see whether they have checked in today ("Checked in at 9:42" or "Not checked in yet").

### Needs your attention

Items waiting for your decision appear here first, for example a leave request, an expense claim or an attendance correction from your team. Click an item to open it. The **Open approvals** button takes you to the Approvals screen. This list refreshes every minute. If nothing is pending, the card is hidden.

### Birthdays & Anniversaries (next 7 days)

Doctors and colleagues who have a birthday or anniversary in the next 7 days, nearest first. For the full list and filters, open **Birthdays & Anniversaries** from the sidebar (section 3).

### Quick actions

Small tiles for everyday tasks:

- **View Calendar:** opens the appointments and visits calendar in a large window (see below).
- **My Day**, **Approvals**, **Tour Plan**, **My Stock**, **Ledger**, **Apply Leave**, **New Expense:** take you straight to that screen. A tile only shows if your role can use that screen.

### Calendar (View Calendar)

The calendar is not shown on the dashboard all the time. Click **View Calendar** to open it. This keeps the dashboard fast.

- Teal boxes are **appointments**. Blue boxes are **visits**. Admins and Managers also see the MR's name on each box.
- **Click a day** to book a new appointment on that day.
- **Drag a pending appointment** to another day or time to move it.
- **Click an appointment or visit** to see its details. From there you can complete the visit, reschedule or cancel.
- To close the calendar, click outside it or press **Esc**. If another window is open on top of it, Esc closes only the top one.

### Number cards

Each card shows a count and how much it changed compared with yesterday. Click a card to open the full screen for that item.

- **Admin / Manager see:** Total Doctors, Total MRs, Today's Appointments, Today's Visits, Pending, Completed and Cancelled Appointments, Completed Visits, Medicine Stock, Medicine Distribution, Monthly Sales, Company Total Sales.
- **MR sees:** Today's Appointments, Today's Visits, Pending Follow-ups, Doctors Assigned, Samples Remaining, Attendance, Monthly Performance, Pending Appointments.

### Charts (last 30 days)

- **Visits & appointments:** visits and appointments per day.
- **Sales by day:** sales per day.
- **Doctor coverage:** how many doctors were reached.
- **Leave mix:** approved leave by type.
- **Top sampled medicines:** the products given most as samples.

### Insights (Admin / Manager)

Four short lists at the bottom: **Top performing MR** (with visits and sales), **Top Prescribed / Sampled Medicines**, **MR-wise Sales** and **Medicine-wise Sales**.

## 3. Birthdays & Anniversaries

This screen lists upcoming birthdays and anniversaries of doctors and team members, so you can wish them on the right day. If someone's day is today, the top of the page says so, for example "2 occasions today".

### What you see

- The list is **grouped by month**: this month first, then the next month.
- Each line shows the name, a detail line (the doctor's speciality and area, or the employee's designation), the type of occasion and how many days are left (**Today**, **Tomorrow** or **In 5 days**). Today's occasions are highlighted in green.
- There are three types: **Birthday**, **Anniversary** (for example "5th anniversary") and **Work anniversary** (for example "3 years in the company"). Age is never shown for birthdays.
- **Click a name** to open that doctor's or employee's profile.
- **Click the phone button** to call them directly from a mobile phone.

### How to filter

- **All / Doctors / Team:** choose everyone, only doctors or only team members. Each tab shows a count.
- **Search:** type a name, speciality or area.
- **Range:** Next 7 days, Next 30 days, Next 3 months or Full year.

### Where the dates come from

A doctor's birthday and anniversary are entered when adding or editing the doctor on the **Doctors** screen (or through the Excel import). Team members' dates come from their profile on the **Employees** screen. If a date is not filled in, the person does not appear here.

## 4. Field — My Day, Approvals, Tour Plan, Attendance

### My Day (MR / Manager)

Open this first before going to the field. It shows all of today's work in one place.

- **Attendance:** if you have not checked in, it says "Check in with GPS before your first call". The **Check in** button takes you to the Attendance screen.
- **Today's plan:** from your approved Tour Plan — today's beat / HQ, type of work and your call list. Each doctor or chemist has a phone number. Progress shows as "3/8 calls done".
- **Today's appointments:** appointments booked for today.
- **Pending follow-ups:** visits whose follow-up date has arrived.
- Small buttons at the top open Tour plan, All appointments, Visits, **New appointment** and Apply leave.

If your tour plan is not approved, you will see "No approved plan for today". Make and submit your Tour Plan first.

### Approvals (Admin / Manager)

All team requests waiting for your decision. "Awaiting you" shows the total. There are four parts:

1. **Leave requests:** who asked for leave, what type, which dates and how many working days. Click **Approve** or **Reject**. You can add a remark, for example "Approved — Amit will cover your beat". Once approved, those days are marked as leave in the attendance register.
2. **Flagged check-ins:** check-ins the system was not sure about: weak GPS, fake (mock) location, or the phone's clock not matching the server. Talk to the person, then **Accept** the check-in or reject it. If you reject it, that day is marked **Absent**.
3. **Regularizations:** requests to fix a missed check-in or check-out. Approving corrects the attendance register.
4. **Expense claims:** your team's TA/DA claims (category, amount, distance). Approve or reject them here. The payment itself is handled on the Expenses screen.

### Tour Plan (MTP)

Your monthly tour plan: where you will go each day and which doctors and chemists you will call on.

1. Pick the month with the ← → buttons. Click a day on the calendar.
2. Choose the **Work type**: Field, Office, Joint work, Leave or Holiday.
3. Choose the **Beat / HQ**. For joint work, also pick the colleague.
4. Use **Add doctor** and **Add chemist** to add that day's calls. You can write notes, for example "Camp at district hospital".
5. Click **Save day**. To start the day again, click **Clear day**.
6. When the whole month is ready, click **Submit for approval**. After you submit, the plan is **locked** until your manager decides.

The plan status shows at the top: **Draft**, **Waiting for your manager**, **Approved** (and by whom) or **Rejected** (with the manager's remark). You can fix a rejected plan and submit it again. An approved plan becomes your daily call list in **My Day**.

Managers have three tabs: **My plan** (their own), **Inbox** (plans submitted by the team — click **View** to approve or reject) and **Team plans** (everyone's plans).

### Attendance

GPS attendance, the monthly register and corrections.

- **Check in / Check out:** when you click the button, the browser asks for your location — click **Allow**. The screen shows your in and out time, hours worked and GPS accuracy (for example ±25 m).
- **Register:** a colour-coded calendar for the month: Present, Late, Absent, Leave, Holiday / weekly off and Not marked. Approved leave and holidays fill in automatically. Click a day to see its full details.
- **All entries:** every attendance entry in a list, with search.
- **Regularize a day:** if you missed a check-in or check-out in the **last 31 days**, send a request from here with the reason. When your manager approves it, the register is corrected.
- **Manager / Admin:** choose a team member at the top to open their register. Use **Mark / correct attendance** to enter or fix a day yourself, for example an office day or joint work. Days with approved leave can only be changed by cancelling the leave.

## 5. Field — Leave, Expenses, Appointments, Visits / DCR

### Leave

Apply for leave, check your balance and approve your team's leave. Approved leave is added to attendance automatically.

- Three boxes at the top: **Paid entitlement** (how much leave you get in a year), **Used** (how much you took) and **Awaiting approval** (how much is pending).
- **Apply for leave:** choose the leave type, then the duration: **Full day(s)**, **First half**, **Second half** or **Short leave (hours)** (only where that type allows it). Enter the from / to dates, the reason, a contact number while you are away and, if needed, a document. Click **Submit request**.
- **Sundays and company holidays in between are not counted.** The form shows how many working days you are taking.
- **My requests:** all your requests and their status. You can cancel a pending request here.
- **Team requests** and **Team calendar** (Manager / Admin): your team's requests and a calendar of who is on leave on which day. Approve or reject from the Approvals screen, or open a request here with **View**.

### Expenses (TA/DA)

Travel and daily allowance claims, with a photo of the bill. The manager approves and the Admin pays.

- Three boxes at the top: **Claimed this month**, **Approved** (payment pending) and **Paid out** (payment received).
- **New claim:** enter the date, category (**Travel (TA)**, **Daily allowance (DA)**, Hotel / stay, Food, Phone / internet, Stationery, Other), amount (₹), distance (km) and a description, for example "Bike: HQ → Sikandra beat → HQ (34 km)". Upload a photo of the bill or receipt. Click **Submit claim**.
- You can edit or delete a claim while it is **Pending**.
- A claim has one of four statuses: **Pending**, **Approved**, **Paid**, **Rejected**. Use ← → to change the month.
- **Team claims** (Manager / Admin): your team's claims. Approval happens on the Approvals screen. After paying an approved claim, the Admin clicks **Mark paid**.

### Appointments

Book a meeting with a doctor and complete it after the meeting.

- **New Appointment:** fill in the doctor, date, time, purpose, place (clinic branch), priority and remarks. Admins and Managers can use **Assign to MR** to give the appointment to an MR.
- The list shows date, time, doctor, MR, purpose and status. Use the search box and the status filter: All, **Pending**, **Completed**, **Cancelled**, **Rescheduled**.
- Open an appointment to do one of three things:
    - **Reschedule:** pick a new date or time.
    - **Cancel:** cancel it after confirming (this cannot be undone).
    - **Complete + Visit:** after the meeting, fill in the visit report (DCR) right here (see below).
- To see appointments on a calendar, use **View Calendar** on the Dashboard.

### Visits / DCR

A visit is created only by completing an appointment (**Complete + Visit**). The form has:

- **Visit time:** date, check-in and check-out time. The meeting length is calculated for you.
- **Meeting:** what was discussed, the doctor's feedback, the outcome, the **next follow-up date** and remarks.
- **Products discussed:** which medicines you talked about, the doctor's interest level and the expected prescriptions.
- **Sample distribution:** which sample, batch and quantity you gave. This quantity is **taken out of the MR's stock automatically**.

The Visits / DCR screen lists all visits: date, doctor, MR, duration, outcome and follow-up. Search the list and click **View** to see the full details. MRs see only their own visits. Admins and Managers see everyone's and can delete a wrong entry.

## 6. People — Employees, Holiday Calendar, Leave Policy

### Employees (Admin / Manager)

The HR file of every MR and Manager: who they report to, their attendance and their remaining leave.

- Counts at the top: **Total employees**, **Managers**, **Medical reps** and **Inactive**. Use the tabs to see everyone, only Managers or only MRs.
- Search by name, employee code, area or manager.
- The list shows code, role, reporting manager, time in the company, this month's attendance and **Leave left**.
- New accounts are not created here. Create MR and Manager accounts on the **Users & Hierarchy** screen.

**Click an employee** to open their full profile. It has five tabs:

1. **Overview:** this month's attendance, remaining leave, visits, POB (sales), assigned doctors, samples given, territory and beat.
2. **Attendance:** the full year — Present, Late, Absent and Leave days, flagged check-ins and hours worked.
3. **Leave:** each leave type — how much they get, have used, have pending and have left. You can also **Grant comp-off** here.
4. **Employment & bank:** employee code, designation, joining date, reporting manager, PAN, Aadhaar and bank details (used for TA/DA payments).
5. **Documents:** upload papers like Aadhaar, PAN or the offer letter (**Add document**).

Use **Edit record** to change personal details: date of birth, anniversary, blood group, qualification, address and emergency contact. These dates appear on the Birthdays & Anniversaries screen.

**Reset link (Admin):** if an employee forgets their password, create a **reset link** from their profile. The link works **only once** and is valid for **60 minutes**. Give it to the employee in a safe way — it is shown only once.

### Holiday Calendar (Admin)

The list of company and regional holidays. These days are not counted as leave and are shown as holidays in the attendance register.

- **Add holiday:** enter the date, name (for example Diwali), type and a note. Types: **National**, **Festival**, **Regional**, **Company** or **Weekly off**.
- **Optional / restricted** holidays are not applied automatically. Employees apply for them like leave.
- View the holidays as a **Calendar** or as a **Full year list**. Use the buttons to move between years.
- You can **Edit** or delete any holiday. Deleting a holiday does not recalculate leave that was already approved.
- Add the whole year's holidays at the start of the year so leave counts stay correct.

### Leave Policy (Admin)

Here you decide the types of leave and how much leave each person gets.

- **New leave type:** a code (for example CL, SL, EL), a name, the yearly quota (days) and the rules:
    - **Paid / Unpaid**
    - **Carry forward:** how much leave moves to next year
    - Whether **Half day** and **Short leave** are allowed
    - **Sandwich rule:** whether a Sunday or holiday between two leave days also counts as leave
    - A colour, used for this type on calendars
- A quota of **0** means there is no yearly limit (for example LWP or comp-off).
- Only a leave type that has never been used can be deleted. If it has been used, make it **Inactive** instead.
- **Per-employee entitlement:** set one employee's leave separately — their opening balance and extra leave granted this year. The counts at the top show active types, paid days per year and carry-forward types.

## 7. Masters — Users, Manager Access, Doctors, Territories, Stores, Products

### Users & Hierarchy (Admin)

Create logins for MRs and Managers (ASM / RSM) and set who reports to whom.

- **Add employee:** first choose the account type — **Medical Representative** or **Manager (ASM / RSM)**. Then enter the name, employee code, designation, phone, beat / area, joining date, address, reporting manager, work email and a **starting password** (at least 8 characters). HR details like PAN and bank can be added later on the Employees screen.
- The list shows code, name, role, reporting manager, area and status, with a search box.
- For each user: **Deactivate / Activate** (a deactivated user cannot log in), **Reset password** (set a new password yourself) and **Delete**. Before deleting someone who has people reporting to them, move those people to another manager.

### Manager Access (Admin)

Managers get a default set of screens (see the table in section 1). To give one Manager more or less access:

- Each Manager in the list is marked **Defaults** or **Custom**.
- Open a Manager, tick or untick permissions and click **Save permissions**. The change applies immediately.
- To go back to the normal set, click **Apply default set**.

### Doctors

The doctor list follows the company's MSL sheet. Each doctor has these fields: **MSL No** (given by the system), **Area** with HQ / EX / Out-station, **Doctor's Name**, **Mobile No**, **MSL Class**, **Speciality**, **Address**, **Email ID**, **Birthday** and **Anniversary**.

- **MSL No:** the system gives every new doctor the next number — JL-001, JL-002, JL-003 and so on. Nobody types or changes it, so two doctors can never have the same MSL No.
- **List:** sorted by MSL No, smallest first. Search by MSL No, name, mobile, area or speciality. MRs see only the doctors assigned to them.
- **Favourite (star):** every doctor has a star. Click it — the star turns yellow and the doctor moves to the **top of the list**. Each user has their own favourites.
- **Actions:** the blue eye button **Open** (the doctor's full file), the amber pencil button **Edit**, and the red bin button **Delete** (after a confirmation).
- **Add Doctor (Admin):** fill in the fields above (MSL No is added for you when you save) and, if you want, assign an MR. If a doctor with the **same name and same area** already exists, the app shows an error so you do not create a duplicate.
- **Import Excel (Admin):** click **Import Excel** and choose your MSL sheet. The columns should be: AREA-HQ/EX, DOCTOR'S NAME, MOBILE NO, MSLCLASS, SPECIALITY, ADDRESS, EMAIL ID, BIRTHDAY, ANNIVERSARY. A MYMSL NO column can stay in the sheet, but it is ignored — each doctor gets a system MSL No. The doctors are added as soon as you pick the file, and a message shows the result, for example "120 doctors added · 8 duplicates skipped". Doctors whose name and area are already in the list are skipped. A cell like "BORING ROAD-HQ" is read as Area = Boring Road and type = HQ.
- **Doctor's file (Open):** the top shows the name, MSL No, speciality and area, and an **Add to favourites** button. Below are tabs: **Overview** (profile and history), **Appointments**, **Visits**, **Medicines** (how often each medicine was discussed), **Sample History**, **Billing** (invoices) and **Reports**.

### Territories (Admin)

The structure of your areas: **State → District → HQ → Beat**. Beats are used in the Tour Plan. The centre of an HQ or beat is used for the attendance geo-fence.

- Create a State first, then add Districts, HQs and Beats under it. Each line has a **+** button to add the next level.
- Each territory has a name and a code (for example LKO-01), and a **geo-fence**: latitude, longitude and radius (in metres). A check-in outside this circle is flagged on the Approvals screen.
- Each line has **Edit** and **Delete**. A territory that still has other territories or employees under it cannot be deleted.

### Chemists / Stores (Admin / Manager)

A directory of partner medical stores.

- **Add Medical Store:** store name, owner / pharmacist, GST number, drug license (DL) number, phone and city.
- The list shows store, owner, GST, license and city, with search. These stores are added as chemist calls in the Tour Plan, and bills are raised to them in Sales.

### Products (Admin / Manager)

The master list of medicines: brand, generic, batch, expiry, stock and samples.

- **Add Medicine:** name, brand, generic / salt, company, composition, strength, category, first batch number, expiry date, MRP (₹), opening stock, the **low stock alert** level and whether samples are available for MRs.
- **Import (Excel / CSV):** add many medicines at once. You first see a preview: which are new, which will be updated and which have errors. The quantity in the file is **added** to the warehouse stock (old stock is not replaced). **Download template** gives you a file in the right format.
- **Details:** the full story of one medicine — Overview, **MR Holdings** (how much each MR has), **MR-wise Usage**, **Doctor-wise Distribution** and the **Distribution Timeline**.

## 8. Stock — My Stock, Issue to MR, Sample Given, Stock Balances, Ledger Explorer

Samples move like this: **warehouse (company stock) → Issue to MR → MR's bag (My Stock) → sample given to a doctor during a visit (Sample Given)**. Every step is recorded in the ledger.

### My Stock (MR / Manager)

The samples you are carrying right now.

- Counts at the top: **Units in bag**, number of products, and **Expiring in 60 days** (give these out first).
- **Holdings:** the remaining quantity of each product and batch.
- **Recent movement:** your last 30 movements — stock you received, samples you gave and returns.
- If your bag is empty you will see "Your bag is empty". Stock appears here as soon as your manager issues it.

### Issue to MR (Admin / Manager)

Send samples from the warehouse to an MR's bag.

- **Issue Sample Stock to MR:** choose the MR, medicine, quantity, batch number, date and a remark or voucher (for example "Monthly sample allocation").
- The list shows the issue number, date, MR, medicine, batch, quantity and remark, with search. MRs only see what was issued to them.

### Sample Given

A record of samples given to doctors. You do not enter this separately — the samples you record **while completing a visit** appear here, and the MR's stock goes down automatically. The list shows date, medicine, quantity, batch, doctor and MR, with search.

### Stock Balances (Admin / Manager)

The warehouse position for each medicine: **Opening + Received − Issued to MR − Write-off = Available**. A warning shows when stock falls below the **Min Alert** level.

- **Adjust:** if a physical count does not match or a batch is damaged, add stock (for example +50) or remove it (for example −10), and always write the reason. Every adjustment is recorded in the ledger.

### Ledger Explorer (Admin / Manager)

A record of every stock movement: when stock came in, who added it and where it went. Nothing in this record is ever erased. It has three tabs:

1. **Ledger:** all movements. Filter by date (From / To), medicine, transaction type or search. The top shows units in, units out and the number of movements. Use **Export CSV** to open it in Excel.
2. **Receipts (GRN):** a GRN is created every time stock comes in (through a medicine import or a positive adjustment) — date, source, invoice number, file, total units and who created it. Open a receipt to see all its lines.
3. **Statement:** the monthly account — received, issued, written off / sold. **Mismatches** lists medicines whose account does not match the balance.

## 9. Commercial — Sales / POB, Reports

### Sales / POB

Bills to doctors and chemists, payments received, quick sale entries and monthly targets — all in one place. There are four tabs:

1. **Invoices:** all bills — invoice number, date, party (doctor or chemist), MR, total, amount paid and amount due. Filter by status, date or search. There are four statuses: **Unpaid** (red), **Partial** (yellow), **Paid** (green) and **Cancelled**.
    - **New Invoice:** choose a Doctor or Chemist, the MR who booked the order, the invoice date and the due date. Then add lines: medicine, quantity, rate (₹) and discount (%). You can write payment terms in Remarks. Click **Save Invoice**. The invoice lines are also added to sales automatically.
    - **Open a bill** to see all lines, subtotal, discount, total, amount paid and amount due. **Record a payment** with the amount, date, mode (Cash, UPI, Cheque, Bank transfer, Credit note, Other) and a reference (UPI transaction or cheque number). If you delete a wrong payment, the amount due is recalculated.
    - **Cancel invoice:** the bill is marked CANCELLED and its sales are removed from all reports.
2. **Quick POB:** a quick sale entry without a full bill — date, medicine, quantity, amount and the doctor or medical store. Click **Save Sale**.
3. **Party outstanding:** how much each doctor or chemist still owes. View **Doctor-wise** or **Chemist-wise**: total billed, total paid, total due and the last invoice date. Use **Export CSV** to open it in Excel.
4. **Targets:** monthly target vs achieved. Managers and Admins use **Set sales target** to set an employee's target — amount (₹), units, and optionally a separate target for one medicine. Each employee has a progress bar. MRs see their own target and achievement. Booked sales still count even if no target is set.

### Reports

Ready-made reports that you can filter and download as Excel or PDF.

1. Choose the **Report type**.
    - **For Admin / Manager:** Daily, Weekly, Monthly, MR Performance, MR Detail, Doctor Visit, Appointment, Medicine Distribution, Sales and Stock Report.
    - **For MR (own data only):** My Daily, My Monthly, My Performance, My Visit, My Appointment, My Sample and My Sales Report.
2. Apply filters: **From / To** date, MR, doctor, medicine, medical store and status (whichever apply to that report).
3. Click **Export Excel** or **Export PDF**. The PDF opens in a print window, where you can save or print it.

## 10. System — Settings, Audit Log (Admin)

### Settings

App settings for the whole company. These are usually set up once.

- **Branding:** the company name and identity, taken from company documents.
- **Settings list:** each setting has a **Key**, a **Group** and a **Value**.
- **Add or change a setting:** enter the key, group and value and click **Save setting**. For example, `stock.default_warehouse_id` tells the app which warehouse receives stock from a medicine import.
- If you are not sure what a setting does, ask your IT team or developer before changing it. A wrong value can break stock or attendance calculations.

### Audit Log

A record of who did what and when in the app, for security and checking.

- Each line shows **When**, **User** (who), **Action** (what they did — for example login, import, delete), **Entity** (on what) and **IP** (from where).
- Every successful login is recorded automatically.
- Use the search box to find entries for a user, an action or an item.
